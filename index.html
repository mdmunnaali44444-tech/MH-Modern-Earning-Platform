MH / LEGAL

শর্তাবলি

Fair use

Members must provide accurate account information, follow each provider's eligibility and completion rules, and must not use bots, duplicate accounts, fake traffic, or manipulated activity. Provider decisions and reward verification may take time.

Rewards and withdrawals

Rewards are credited only after trusted provider verification. No earning amount, task availability, or payout time is guaranteed. Withdrawal methods, minimums and processing times will be published only after payment partners and operational rules are confirmed.

Account actions

MH may investigate suspicious activity and restrict accounts under published rules. Members may contact support to ask about an account decision.

গোপনীয়তা নীতি

Information

MH uses account information needed to provide sign-in, profile, task and support features. Do not submit passwords, payment PINs, private keys or wallet recovery phrases in support messages.

Use and security

Account records are stored using Firebase services configured for this project. Access should be limited by Firestore Security Rules and trusted server workflows. This page must be reviewed and updated before public launch to reflect actual providers, analytics, retention and contact details.

Contact
